Frequently asked questions

How do I restore my purchases?
Open Settings in the game and tap “Restore purchases”.

How can I change my advertising choices?
Open Settings in the game and tap “Privacy preferences”.

Is my progress saved?
Yes, progress is saved on your device. Deleting the app removes it.
